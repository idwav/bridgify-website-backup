Frequently asked questions

What's the best approach to integrating multiple experience providers into my platform?

Use a multi-source experiences provider instead of building and maintaining separate supplier APIs. Mapping, de-duplication, availability sync and booking flows are handled once, upstream, and your team keeps building your core product.

Is there a travel agency API for tours and activities?

Yes. The Bridgify Experiences API gives travel agency software and agent tools access to tours, attractions, activities and events, with real-time availability, pricing and booking.

What are the most reliable B2B tours distribution platforms?

Look for broad supply, real-time availability and pricing, and enterprise support. A single-marketplace API depends on one catalog. A multi-source provider such as Bridgify, part of HBX Group, brings several suppliers together behind one integration, so one missing source doesn't leave a gap.

Can tour operators add activities to their packages?

Yes, through the API or a branded store, with no new supplier contracts per destination.

Is Bridgify a reservation system for tour operators?

No. Reservation systems help operators manage their own bookings and channels. Bridgify sits on the other side: it connects supply that's already distributed to the platforms that sell it.
