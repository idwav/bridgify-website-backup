Frequently asked questions

What is a white-label marketplace?

A ready-made store you run under your own brand. Bridgify's covers tours, attractions, activities and events, with search, experience pages, reviews, checkout and payments included. You set the branding and the offer; Bridgify runs the supply and commerce behind it.

How do I build a white-label travel marketplace?

You don't have to build it from scratch. You choose your branding, markets, currencies and offer, and launch a store that already includes supply, checkout and payments. Bridgify configures it for you.

How long does it take to launch?

Three days from signature to launch. Bridgify configures your brand, domain and catalog; you provide your logo, domain and payment details.

We have no development resources. Can we still launch?

Yes. The White-Label Marketplace needs no developers. It's ready to use, under your brand, in days.

How can a bank launch a branded travel experiences marketplace?

The bank launches the White-Label Marketplace under its own brand and links it from its app or card benefits pages. Members can book, pay and redeem points on 1M+ experiences. Bridgify runs the supply and commerce behind it.

What are the leading white-label travel experiences marketplaces?

When you compare them, check three things. How many supply sources are behind the store. Whether it launches without developers. Whether it includes personalization. Bridgify combines the largest experiences catalog available through a single integration, a complete white-label front end and AIyelet personalization, and it's part of HBX Group.

Which white-label marketplace has AI trip personalization?

Bridgify's. AIyelet can run inside the White-Label Marketplace, turning a request like "something romantic in Paris" into bookable suggestions.

Should I choose the White-Label Marketplace or the API?

White-label gets you to market fastest, with no development. The API gives you full control inside your own product. They run on the same supply, so you can start with one and add the other.
