How we started

We started because selling experiences was close to impossible.

Amit Shamni and Yaniv Green founded Bridgify in 2019. Every experience supplier worked differently: different booking systems, prices, cancellation rules and ticket formats. Selling experiences at scale meant connecting them one by one. We built the infrastructure that makes them work as one.

Our first partners weren’t travel companies. They were credit card companies and loyalty programs, with millions of customers who travel and a clear wish to give them more value on the trip. Working with them taught us what still shapes the product: how to handle many currencies, how loyalty points work, and how to give members something worth redeeming.

Our mission

From fragmented supply to the right experience for every customer.

Travel experiences are spread across suppliers, systems and markets. We connect that supply, normalize it into one schema and make it bookable through one integration, inside our partners’ own channels. Then AIyelet matches each customer to the experiences that fit, so a million options become the right few.

Our mission, in one line: make the world’s experiences usable at scale for any partner that wants to sell them, matched to the right traveler at the right moment.
