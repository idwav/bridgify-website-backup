ABOUT BRIDGIFY

Connecting global experiences to the brands people use.

Bridgify provides the supply, commerce infrastructure, and personalization that brands need to offer travel experiences within their own products.

01 — STORY & MISSION

Making a fragmented market easier to work with.

Travel experiences are spread across suppliers, systems, and markets. We connect that supply and normalize its content so brands can offer and sell experiences through one integration.

02 — HBX GROUP

Experience infrastructure backed by HBX Group.

As part of HBX Group, Bridgify brings its expertise in travel experiences to a global travel technology group.
