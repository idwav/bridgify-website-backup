Frequently asked questions

How do I add tours and activities to a super app?

Connect to an experiences infrastructure provider instead of individual suppliers. With Bridgify, a super app integrates one travel booking API, or launches a branded store, to offer more than one million tours, attractions, activities and events. Bridgify handles supply, content and booking.

How can fintechs offer bookable tours in-app?

Embed the Bridgify Experiences API so users search, book and pay for tours without leaving the app. For a faster launch, use the White-Label Marketplace with payments and checkout built in.

How do super apps embed travel experiences?

As another in-app service. Destination, location and user context surface relevant tours and tickets, and checkout runs through the app's own payment flow.

Can users stay in our app all the way through checkout?

Yes, with the API. Your team builds the interface and checkout inside the app, and Bridgify provides availability, pricing and booking behind it.

Is there an attractions API for super apps and digital wallets?

Yes. The Experiences API covers attraction tickets as well as tours, activities and events, from multiple suppliers.
