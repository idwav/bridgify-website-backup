Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

What is a destination experiences API?

An API that gives an airline or travel brand bookable tours, attractions, activities and events at the passenger's destination. Bridgify's combines multiple suppliers behind one integration, so airlines sell experiences in their own channels without contracting local providers.

What are the alternatives to a single-supplier destination experiences API?

Airlines can contract one marketplace's API, build supplier connections in-house, or connect to a multi-source provider. Bridgify is the third option: multiple marketplaces, aggregators and suppliers in one normalized API, for wider destination coverage through one integration.

How can airlines make ancillary revenue from tours and activities?

Offer destination experiences where passengers already are: the booking flow, confirmation emails, manage-my-booking and the app. With Bridgify, airlines sell 1M+ experiences under their own brand without contracting suppliers or building the infrastructure.

Where in the passenger journey should airlines offer experiences?

At booking, while the trip is being planned. Before departure, when passengers research the destination. And on arrival, in the app.

Can loyalty members redeem miles for experiences?

Yes. Experiences can sit inside your loyalty program, so members can earn and redeem miles on them. Bridgify connects the supply behind your branded experience.

Do we need to change our booking engine?

No. Embed experiences through the API in your existing channels, or launch a branded experiences store that links from them.
