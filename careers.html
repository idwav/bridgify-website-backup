Why now

Travel experiences are where traveltech gets decided next

The supply of tours, activities and attractions is split across many suppliers. Banks, airlines and super apps want it inside their own products, and AI is changing what the infrastructure underneath has to do. It is being built right now.

  1. 1

    Supply is split across many suppliers

    Every source has its own format, its own system and its own way of saying what is available. Someone has to make them one thing.

    SuppliersAggregatorsMarketplacesDirect sources
  2. 2

    Banks, airlines and super apps want it in their product

    The biggest platforms want experiences their customers can book without leaving the app, under their own brand.

    BanksAirlinesSuper apps
  3. 3

    AI is changing what the infrastructure must do

    Understanding what a traveler is asking for, and matching it across all that supply, is being built into the platform now.
