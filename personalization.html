Frequently asked questions

How do AI experience recommendations work?

AIyelet reads traveler intent, profile and context, such as destination and travel style when available. It matches them against more than one million experiences and returns personalized recommendations instead of a generic list.

Isn't a catalog of a million experiences just noise?

Not with AIyelet on top. Bridgify organizes the supply into one clean schema, and AIyelet shows each traveler the few experiences that fit their destination, dates and travel style.

What is intent-based search?

Travelers describe what they want in their own words. "I want to do something romantic in Paris" returns relevant, bookable experiences, without the traveler choosing categories and filters first.

Is there an AI-powered itinerary planner API for travel platforms?

Yes. AIyelet is Bridgify's AI itinerary planner and personalization layer. Given a destination, dates and traveler context, it recommends the experiences that fit and can arrange them into a plan, inside your own product.

Travelers can book experiences inside AI assistants now. Why add them to our own product?

Inside an assistant, the traveler meets the marketplace's brand, and the customer relationship belongs to the marketplace. AIyelet puts the matching inside your product, so you keep the whole trip: the customer, their booking data and the chance to sell them the next experience.

Can I offer personalized travel experiences without building a recommendation engine?

Yes. AIyelet sits on top of Bridgify's supply, so recommendations come through the same integration as the experiences. You don't need to build or train your own model.

Does personalization work with both the API and the White-Label Marketplace?

Yes. AIyelet can power recommendations in your own product through the Experiences API, or inside the White-Label Marketplace under your brand.
