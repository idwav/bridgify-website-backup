Frequently asked questions

How can a bank or loyalty program embed experiences?

Two ways. Launch a branded travel rewards marketplace with search, checkout and payments built in, or embed experiences in your existing app through an API. Bridgify provides both, with 1M+ tours, attractions, activities and events from multiple global suppliers behind one integration.

How do I add experiences redemption to a rewards program?

Connect your program to an experiences catalog that accepts your points. With Bridgify, members redeem points on 1M+ experiences inside your rewards flow, and your loyalty engine keeps managing points, tiers and members.

What is the best platform to monetize cardholder travel experiences?

Look for broad global supply, a branded customer experience, payments and checkout built in, and enterprise reliability. Bridgify, part of HBX Group, gives financial institutions the largest experiences catalog available through a single integration, through a white-label marketplace or an API.

What are the best secure experience APIs for financial institutions?

Look for an enterprise provider with a track record in financial services, KYC support, secure payment handling and clear SLAs. Bridgify started with card issuers and loyalty programs and works with banks and card companies today.

Does Bridgify replace our loyalty program software?

No. Bridgify adds an experiences catalog to the loyalty platform you already run. Your loyalty engine manages points, tiers and members; Bridgify supplies and manages the experiences.

What are the alternatives to building a travel API for banks and loyalty platforms?

Instead of contracting suppliers and building integrations in-house, connect once to an experiences infrastructure provider. Bridgify aggregates multiple suppliers, normalizes the content and handles booking, so your team builds only the customer journey, or none of it with white-label.
