Frequently asked questions

How can hotels sell tours and activities to guests?

Add bookable local experiences to the channels guests already use: pre-arrival emails, the booking confirmation, the hotel app and in-room screens. With Bridgify, hotels offer 1M+ tours, attractions, activities and events under their own brand.

How can hotels increase ancillary revenue with local experiences?

Every booked tour, ticket or activity earns the hotel revenue, without adding staff or supplier contracts. It adds a revenue line from the destination itself, next to room upsells.

Can Bridgify integrate with our PMS or guest app?

You don’t need an integration. The simplest route for hotels is the White-Label Marketplace: a branded experiences store you link from your guest app, website, pre-arrival emails and in-room screens, live in 3 days. Hotel-tech providers that want experiences inside their own product can use the Experiences API.

Do we need staff to manage experience bookings?

No. Bridgify handles supplier connections, content, availability, booking and customer service.

Do you work with independent hotels?

We work with hotel groups and with the technology providers that serve independent and boutique hotels. If you run an independent property, ask your guest-app or booking-engine provider about Bridgify.

Plan your hotel's experiences offering.

Tell us about your properties, destinations and guest journey. We'll recommend the supply and product that fit.
