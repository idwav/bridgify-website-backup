01 · Brand and voice

The partner's brand comes first.

Everything Bridgify says comes back to one page of messages. If a line feels unclear, check it against these.

What we are

The infrastructure behind travel experiences. Bridgify, part of HBX Group.

How we describe ourselves

The world's leading B2B travel experiences infrastructure. A B2B traveltech company.

Never "platform" or "aggregator" when describing Bridgify.

What it means

One integration lets a brand sell 1M+ tours, attractions, activities and events in 180+ countries in its own channels, matched to each customer. Bridgify runs the supply, distribution and personalization. The brand keeps the customer.

Four capabilities

  1. 01AggregateThe largest experiences catalog available through a single integration: the major marketplaces, aggregators and direct suppliers. Keep the suppliers you know, add the rest.
  2. 02NormalizeEvery source mapped and de-duplicated into one schema. One integration instead of one per supplier.
  3. 03DistributeInto the partner's own channels, under the partner's brand. Bookings, payments, currencies and fulfillment handled. The partner owns the customer.
  4. 04PersonalizeAIyelet matches the right experience to the right traveler, using intent and context, inside the partner's product.

Proof we can use

Voice

Short, direct sentences. Numbers instead of adjectives. The partner's brand comes first. No hype words, no em dashes.

  • American English. Short declarative sentences, a period at the end of headings (not list headings such as "Who we work with").
  • The customer is at the centre: "your customers", "your brand", "your product". Bridgify is the infrastructure behind.
  • Figures always carry a short label, and a source when they belong to one product.
  • Never invent numbers, clients, logos, quotes, pricing, team members or URLs. Demos are labelled "Illustrative product preview".
  • Copy v4 is the only source of text.

Two-part headlines

A statement, then a turn. The turn takes the coral ink. The ink goes on the words that carry the customer's benefit, never on the brand name. One heading, one accent.

  • Your customers already book experiences. Just not with you.
  • Don't give travelers a library. Give them the right book.
  • Three products. One connected layer.
  • Built for brands that already have the traveler

08 · Illustration

The content draws the picture.

Before drawing, write in one sentence what the visitor needs to understand. Every wire, layer and object has a job in that explanation.

  • CablesCoral, on smooth, directed paths with few turns. They converge into one bar of dots and branch out from it. They never cross text.
  • Data dotsSmall warm-white circles (r≈3) with a coral glow, moving along cables and breathing. Round, never dashes or capsules. No dot sits off a cable.
  • Contact pointsA cream circle with a coral line, only where a cable touches a part.
  • DevicesPhone, laptop and tablet with a glowing coral edge and a dark screen that lights up.
  • Interface mockupsExperience cards with warm travel photos, rounded corners and a coral button.
  • Glass layers and dot gridsTranslucent surfaces in perspective; a fine matrix of dots with a few lit in coral.

Material and light

  • A small side thickness, a narrow lit edge, a contact shadow and a soft reflection.
  • A dark body with a coral edge, or a cream body with a warm shadow.
  • Local light only: no neon, gold or dramatic corner light.
  • Every glass surface sits on an opaque floor.
  • {{RULE_ILLUSTRATION}}

Not in the language: a globe by default, blue, satellites or stars, filled or multicolored icons. HTML, SVG and CSS first; text stays live.
