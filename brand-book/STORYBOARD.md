# Bridgify brand book: storyboard v2 (approved by the owner, 25 Nov 2026, including the items marked proposed)

**The book's form.** Each page is a full-screen spread holding one idea. On desktop and tablet the pages snap to the scroll. Phones scroll naturally, one spread per screen. A thin chapter index with a progress bar sits on the side, and the arrow keys turn the page.

**Rhythm.** A dark chapter opener (one sentence, a huge number and title), then 2–5 spreads: either cream with a single visual, or dark with a "living" visual.

**Text load.** No more than 40 words per spread. Visuals rather than paragraphs. All the detailed values (hex codes, sizes) open in a small "specs" drawer, not on the page itself.

**Visual source.** The live V2 site (identical to `main` @ 5eaff27): the hero funnel, the Louvre that collapses into one card, the four steps, the intent demo, the photos in `assets/v2`, the coral wires and data dots.

**Sources.** Copy v4, DESIGN.md, design system v0.6, the live-site audit, and the competitor research (25 Nov 2026). Anything without an approved source is marked **proposed**.
