Frequently asked questions

How do OTAs get one-contract access to experiences?

Through an experiences infrastructure provider such as Bridgify. One commercial agreement and one integration give access to more than one million tours, attractions, activities and events from multiple global suppliers, instead of a contract and an API per marketplace.

How should I evaluate experiences API suppliers that offer multi-source aggregation?

Compare four things: the number and quality of supply sources; how content is mapped and de-duplicated; real-time availability and pricing; and enterprise support and SLAs.

How does an experiences API integrate with booking engines?

It plugs into your existing booking engine as another product line. Your engine calls it for search, availability, pricing and booking, then shows experiences next to flights and hotels in the same checkout, or as an add-on after booking.

What are the best B2B travel API providers for tours, activities and experiences?

It depends on the coverage you need. Single-source APIs give you one marketplace's catalog. A multi-source API such as Bridgify's combines several marketplaces, aggregators and direct suppliers behind one integration, so you get more coverage and less dependence on one source.

Can we keep our existing marketplace connections?

Yes. Many of the marketplaces OTAs already work with are inside the Bridgify catalog. You can keep your direct connections, run both, or move them into one integration.

How can a smaller travel platform compete without a large catalog of tours?

Connect to a multi-source catalog instead of building one. With Bridgify, a smaller platform offers the same 1M+ experiences in 180+ countries as a large one, from day one.
